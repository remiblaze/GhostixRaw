# Ghostix Raw: one-knob rhythmic auto-gate

![Ghostix Raw free one-knob rhythmic auto-gate UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/GhostixRaw/main/ghostixraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob turns any sound into a tempo-synced ghost gate.**

Ghostix Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Ghostix Pro**. It runs an always-on, tempo-synced rhythmic auto-gate (the trance / tech-house ghost gate), driven by a single macro. It is a plain audio insert, no MIDI needed; drop it on a track, set your DAW tempo, and turn the knob.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/GhostixRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`GhostixRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Ghostix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`GhostixRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Ghostix Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
Ghostix Raw is a single-knob rhythmic gate. Drop it on a pad, chord stab, bassline, or full bus, set the tempo in your DAW, and turn one knob:

- **HAUNT**: the one macro. At zero it is clean and out of the way. Turn it up and the sound starts breathing in a tight 1/8-note gate that gets deeper and choppier the further you push. The response is front-loaded, so the start of the sweep is a gentle pulse and the top end is a hard trance gate. Output is level-matched, so it never jumps in volume as you push it.

The gate free-runs from your host tempo (falling back to 120 BPM if the host reports none). No note input required. It is a pure audio effect that ignores MIDI.
